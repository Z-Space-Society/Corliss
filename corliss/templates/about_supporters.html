Supporters

This project is run with the support of Z-Space Arts & Technology Society, a non-profit society in British Columbia. Z-Space operates a multi-disciplinary studio space in downtown Vancouver. Members pay for coworking space and collaborate on events and projects together. The origin of SCN was pooled funding for shared hardware purchases running at Z-Space, which has grown to be a principled approach to community scale AI.

Sponsor

Greyhaven

greyhaven.co

Greyhaven deploys sovereign AI to businesses. They are our preferred business implementation partner for commercial deployments. We collaborate and advise on open source implementations, including the Greywall Sandbox product that secures, observes, and tracks agent connections for your organization.
