{% extends "base.html" %} {% comment %} The cluster console. Two tables that come from two completely different places, and the difference is the point: - Members are Corliss's own cache — what this deployment believes. It can be stale, incomplete, or (in the orphan case) wrong. - Admins are read live from the registry's public roster record. No cache, no credential, nothing to go stale. Reconciliation is what makes the first agree with the record. Both tables show handles with the DID on the cell's title. The handle is a label — `membership.handles_for` resolves it for reading, and the DID stays the thing being displayed *about*, which is why it is one hover away and never absent from the page. The admins table lists only who holds the role now. Departed terms are in the record and are what `Roster.was_admin_at` reads; this table answers the other question, "who can decide membership today". {% endcomment %} {% block title %}Membership — ZAI{% endblock %} {% block content %}

Admins

Who holds cluster admin right now, read live from the registry's public roster.
