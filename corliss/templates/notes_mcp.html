Notes

Your notes, kept on this network. Read and edit them in Obsidian on your laptop and your phone with the SCN Sync plugin.

Connect Obsidian

{% if sync_url %}
  1. In Obsidian, create a new, vault.
  2. In Settings → Community plugins, turn on community plugins
  3. Browse and search for SCN Sync. Install and Enable the plugin.
  4. Paste the sync address below when the plugin asks for it.
  5. Sign in to SCN with your handle and authorize the plugin.
