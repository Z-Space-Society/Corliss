Four fields, two of them read-only, and the split is the point: the DID and the handle come from atproto and Corliss has no say in either, while the name and the email are seeded from the PDS and then belong to the reader. Showing all four together is what makes the two editable ones legible as edits rather than as settings — you can see what they sit beside and what they do not override. **Blank is offered as an action, not left as an omission.** Clearing a field re-arms the fill in `views._upsert_member`, so the next sign-in takes the PDS's value again. That is the only way back to the original once it is edited, and a reader will not guess it, so the hint says it in as many words. No script.
